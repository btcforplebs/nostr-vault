What if your notes lived with you?

Every note you post today lives on somebody else’s relay. Nostr Vault runs one on hardware you hold, mirrors your outbox, inbox, chats and Blossom media to it, and keeps your key encrypted with NIP-49 or out of the app entirely with a NIP-46 bunker. Zaps ride Nostr Wallet Connect, and your on-chain Bitcoin is derived from the same nsec.

One app. Eleven feeds.

Everything Nostr publishes, one tap away.

Nostr is more than short notes. Nostr Vault reads every kind the network speaks and gives each one a home, so you switch between them from a single menu instead of a single firehose.

  • Following — the people you chose, threaded or condensed, your call.
  • Discover & Popular — the second hop of your follow graph, ranked by real people, not reaction spam.
  • Global — the whole network, behind a Web of Trust filter so it fails closed, not open.
  • Reels — a full-screen vertical video feed with likes, replies and zaps while you watch.
  • Articles — long-form posts drawn as articles, with a proper reader.
  • Recipes, Live & Music — zapcooking recipes, NIP-53 live streams with chat, and trending tracks from Wavlake.
  • Media — a grid of every photo, GIF and video from your follows, sorted by date.

Web of Trust

See who vouches for anyone.

Every account on Nostr sits somewhere in your follow graph. Nostr Vault draws it as a globe: you at the centre, and an orange thread for every follow you already trust.

  • Tap a face — the web re-centres on them, so you see who they know that you know.
  • Real counts — how many of your follows reach someone, and how many of theirs you follow too.
  • Filtering that fails closed — Global and Discover only show people your graph can vouch for. Spam stays out, your people stay in.

What’s inside

Built like a relay. Feels like an app.

Your relay, in the app

The same battle-tested Go relay that powers the Haven CLI is compiled into the app as a static library. One process, no helpers, no server to rent. Your notes are stored on your device first and broadcast outward from there.

Keys that never sit in plaintext

Your nsec is encrypted with NIP-49 and the password lives in the system Keychain. Prefer to keep the key off the device entirely? Pair a NIP-46 remote signer by scanning its bunker QR code, on iPhone and Android.

Zaps and a Lightning wallet

Send and receive sats through Nostr Wallet Connect with a live balance, invoice amounts shown before you pay, lightning addresses and a history tab. Zap a note, a reel or a live stream without leaving it.

Your nsec is a Bitcoin wallet

Nostr Vault derives a native Taproot address from your Nostr key and can sweep it on-chain, signed with Schnorr inside the app and broadcast through Mempool. No external wallet, no extra seed.

Web of Trust, on by default

Global feeds are filtered by the trust graph of your follows, so a fresh account starts with a seeded graph instead of the raw firehose. Language filters, mute-list sync and per-account block lists round it out.

Blossom media, hosted by you

A built-in Blossom server stores your photos and videos, mirrors them to the servers you choose and signs each upload once. Paste an image into the composer and it lands on your own storage.

Notifications with no middleman

DMs, mentions, replies, zaps, reactions and reposts are generated on-device from your own relay stream. No push server, no third party, selectable sounds, and taps that open the right note.

Widgets and a real iPad layout

Vault Pulse, Feed Glance, Quick Actions, Sats, Mosaic and Lock Screen widgets on iOS and iPadOS, with home-screen widgets on Android too. On iPad the feed and relay become true columns with a draggable divider.

Native on every platform

SwiftUI on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Jetpack Compose on Android. Six accent themes, one motion vocabulary that honours Reduce Motion, a GIF keyboard in the composer, and a menu-bar status panel on the Mac.

On-chain

Your nsec is your wallet.

Nostr Vault is the first native Nostr client to derive a real on-chain Bitcoin address directly from your Nostr secp256k1 key, and let you sweep it.

Taproot from your key

Pure BIP-341 key-path Taproot (P2TR) derivation, computed in Go by btcsuite/btcd inside the embedded relay. Same private key, a new address, zero external processes.

Schnorr-signed sweeps

Fetches your UTXOs from a Mempool instance, builds a raw Taproot transaction, signs it with Schnorr via key-path spending and broadcasts it, all inside the app.

Live balance and price

The pre-sweep screen loads your spendable balance live from Mempool with a fiat equivalent from the built-in price service. No surprises before you sign.

Two ways to run it

Pocket relay, or a home on the network.

Every build carries the full relay. What changes is who can reach it.

iPhone, iPad & Android — pocket relay

Your device keeps a complete copy of your notes, media and lists, and pushes everything out to the relays you configure. Nothing on the network can reach in to read from your phone, so it is a private backup and a megaphone rather than a public address. On Android you can even swap in another on-device relay such as Citrine.

Mac + domain — full relay

Run it 24/7 on a Mac with a real URL like relay.yourdomain.com, and anyone can connect to read your notes: your own phone, your friends, other relays. Set the Mac as a relay in the mobile app and it syncs your full history once, then keeps you current. Push and pull.

Back it up — take it anywhere

Export your notes as JSONL and your Blossom media as files, straight from the relay dashboard. Import them on another phone or an iPad and your vault is back, nothing lost. No account to recover, no server to ask.

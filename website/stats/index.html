    How it's counted: the apps add a client tag ("Nostr Vault on iOS", "… on Android" and so on) to the notes you post. Once a day we read public notes on public relays and count the people whose notes carry it. We show totals only: no list of people and no tracking in the apps. People who read but never post, and people we haven't come across yet, aren't counted, so the real number is higher.

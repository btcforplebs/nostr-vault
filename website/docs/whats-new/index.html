    Android (build 98) and Mac · iPhone and iPad

    3.2.2

    • Links open inside the app. A link in a post, a bio, an article or a message opens in Nostr Vault's own browser, and a .fips site loads over the mesh, never over the open internet. A mesh page asks before it sends you off the mesh (Leave the mesh?). Turn off Open Links in Nostr Vault under Settings → Privacy to use your normal browser. (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.)
    • Your Devices in Settings. Your Devices lists the Macs, Linux boxes and phones you linked. Your media syncs to all of them, and a Mac keeps a full copy by default. Your vault sends your phone's posts out for you, and the phone sends a post itself if the vault doesn't answer within about 10 seconds. When you sign with a bunker, your posts, photos and files reach your vault without a tap. See My Devices.
    • Media fills in as it lands. The Media tab shows each file the moment it arrives, the photo picker opens full height, and Auto-Mirror runs when you open the app, picking up where a stopped run left off. A copied-in photo keeps the date it was taken. See Media.
    • Drafts follow you. Drafts are saved encrypted to yourself (NIP-37), so only you can read them and they show up on your other devices. Older drafts are re-saved encrypted the first time they load. (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. The web app keeps drafts in the browser only.)
    • Expanded or Threaded. Compact View is gone. The layout button switches a feed between Expanded and Threaded: Expanded turns replies off, Threaded turns them on, and you can still flip replies by hand. A feed you had on Compact opens as Expanded. See Feeds.
    • Feed details. A note, nevent or naddr written in an article shows as a quote card where it sits. Portrait videos get taller tiles, landscape clips turn sideways to fill the screen, and video tiles show the whole frame. Popular, Catch Up and Underrated end with a line saying the list is done. Profile bios have tappable links.
    • Your web, per account. Each account's web of trust is its own follows and the people several of them follow, not every account on the phone added together. See Web of trust.
    • Strangers stay out of the way. Likes and reposts from people outside your web fold into one "from outside your web" line in the Vault list, with the full list one tap away, and no longer count toward the unread badge. Zaps and new followers keep their own lines. Search folds results from outside your web the same way, and a feed set to Everyone shows a banner with Back to your web. Discover leaves out notes from people you already follow and from people outside your web.
    • The wider web builds itself. After first-run setup, the app builds your wider network (the people your follows follow) by itself, so Underrated, Popular, Global and Discover fill in. If Underrated is still empty it says why and offers Build your wider network.
    • Message alerts keep the text private. A message alert says who it's from, not what it says. Show message text under Settings → Notifications puts the text back (off to start). On Android, message alerts come back after a restart or an update without opening the app (not with Amber). See Notification settings.
    • Mac: a new window. A slim rail down the left, a note you open shows in a Thread column beside the list, and Your space sits on the right. Settings has six pages instead of 24: Account, Your Vault, Relays & Media, Backups, Reading and Help. The menu bar panel shows your vault's status at a glance, with Open Nostr Vault, New Note and Start or Restart. See Get started on Mac.
    • iPad: in landscape, Your space is on the left and the app's rail on the right. Panels slide shut to the rail, and a panel you hide keeps its icon on the rail.
    • Quicker to open. The app opens with your saved notes already on screen, and alerts and the unread badge no longer go missing on a cold start.

    Fixes

    • Your wallet link and signer links are kept in the Keychain (Android: in the app's private storage), not in the settings file, and stay out of backups.
    • Android checks that an invoice asks for exactly the amount you chose before your wallet pays it.
    • The vault's red dot clears when you open the Vault dashboard. An unread message lights it again only when a newer one comes in.
    • Media Servers changes are a draft until you tap Save; nothing is published before.
    • A copy to a new device is done only when everything arrived; a partial copy tries again.
    • New in your web leaves out people you muted.
    • Muting a playing video keeps it playing. iPhones with a 120 Hz screen scroll at 120 Hz.
    • The Mac uses much less CPU and memory while the vault runs, and its relay log can no longer grow without limit.
    • A repost counts toward your web only when the reposted note checks out as really from its author.

    App Store and Zapstore · iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and the web

    3.2.0

    Nostr Vault is now on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and on Zapstore for Android (build 76).

    • Link your devices with two scans. Link a device in Mesh settings, or Add under Your devices on the Vault dashboard: one device shows a code, the other scans it, then they swap and check four words. Both devices add each other, so it's done once. No more copying addresses. Mesh settings are now on the Profile tab on iPhone and iPad, and in the profile-picture hold menu everywhere. See Link a device.
    • Copy your whole vault to a new device. When setup asks New to Nostr Vault?, Copy from my other vault links the two and copies your notes, photos and videos across over the mesh, on Wi-Fi or mobile data, with a moving bar and counts. Every account on the old device that can sign comes too, if you tick it. If the old device can't be reached it tries again every 15 seconds, then offers Try again. See Copy your vault.
    • Your new posts reach every linked phone. iPhones and Android phones send new notes and media to your other linked phones, not just to the Mac.
    • A simpler front door. Create an account or Bring an account. Setup asks two questions, then gets out of the way; what's left to import shows on the Vault tab under Still to bring in.
    • Get your old lists back. Moving in now has Look up and back up my lists, on by default: it finds older versions of your follow list and other lists on history relays (the Lazarus spec) and keeps them in your list history. List backups shows who saved each version, and Merge brings back what a buggy app removed. See List backups.
    • Catch Up and Underrated feeds. Catch Up shows the newest post from each person you follow. Underrated finds good posts from people one step out, never your own follows. Both are worked out by your own vault, on the device. (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.) See Feeds.
    • Big media imports. Import media on the Vault dashboard copies the photos and videos from every one of your notes into your vault, writing each file straight to disk, so long videos no longer run the app out of memory. It runs once by itself after your notes come in. See Import media.
    • Videos in messages. The photo button in a conversation sends videos too. (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.)
    • Live now and On this day widgets. Live now shows people you follow who are live, tap to watch. On this day shows your own notes from this date one and two years ago. (iPhone, iPad and Android.)
    • iPad: Your space. A slim rail down the left, with the feeds you pin (Edit rail), and in landscape a column of panels beside the feed: Live Vault, Web of Trust, Messages, My devices, Live now and On this day. Edit it to add, remove and order them.
    • Picture in picture for live streams and feed videos, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, and for live streams on the web. See Picture in picture.
    • Delete Account and Leave Nostr Forever. Delete Account deletes an account from this device and sends nothing. Leave Nostr Forever, under Settings → Advanced, publishes an empty profile and a request to every relay to erase the account, then wipes the device. (iPhone, iPad and Mac.) See Leave Nostr Forever.
    • The share sheet does more. Share to Nostr Vault to make a picture or video post, keep an encrypted private copy, save for later, follow or mute a profile, pay or zap, or send privately. (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.)
    • Bookmarks. Bookmark in a note's ••• menu, and Vault → Bookmarks to read them.
    • Messages moved into the Vault menu, with a Requests tray for people you don't follow and search that runs on the device.
    • Search shows who's Live now at the top.
    • Replies follow your web. Replies, quotes, article comments and live chat are screened by your web of trust, like the feeds.
    • Hide Nostr Vault logo on posts, under Settings → Appearance (off by default).
    • The web app catches up. It now has Your space and the slim rail on a desktop-size window, diVines, Polls, Bookmarks, the Feed Dashboard, Recipe, Highlight and diVine composers, Sell something in Marketplace, the Notifications page, the full Settings pages, Requests and search in Messages, Look up and back up my lists, saving your wallet connection to your account, and sign-in with a password-locked key (I have a password-locked key (ncryptsec)). It refreshes on its own like other Nostr apps.

    Fixes

    • Imports fetch everything: a relay that goes quiet no longer stalls the import, and the result says honestly what came in.
    • Events too big for one database write are saved instead of dropped.
    • Factory Reset waits for an import or a copy to finish, and forgets the old feed and follow list.
    • Backup restore checks the backup is this vault's, and the Mac won't quit mid backup or restore.
    • Paired devices sign over the mesh with their own device key, not your account key.
    • A refused or unsent message never looks sent, and a send carries on if you leave the conversation.
    • Fewer requests to your signer app: one vault sign-in per session.
    • The feed no longer jumps to the top while you read; new posts slide in at the top.
    • Uploads check with the media server first and show its reason if it refuses.
    • The Relays page says why a relay didn't connect.

    • Plebs vs Zombies. My Web on the WoT tab has a Plebs vs Zombies row that opens plebsvszombies.cc, to find follows who have gone quiet. The app itself never unfollows anyone. (iPhone and Android.)
    • Web: the WoT tab now looks like the iPhone's, with My Web and Rebuild your web.

    • Notifications from each account's relays. Replies and mentions are now fetched from the relays each account lists, so a reply sent only there still reaches your Vault list.
    • Message alerts. No more "New message" alerts for messages you sent yourself from another device or account. An alert comes only once the message is unlocked, and only from someone else.
    • Android: the live and music player shows only on the main tabs, not over Settings, messages, the wallet or full-screen photos. Playback goes on while it's hidden. The iPhone already worked this way.
    • Android: a read-only account now says No signing key instead of Local Key.
    • Android: your posts now go to all five of your servers.
    • Web: notifications check about once a minute while the tab is open, and every five minutes while it's in the background.
    • New profiles keep loading until their relays answer, instead of showing up empty.
    • Smoother photo swipes in the feed and in full screen.
    • Fewer requests to your signer app.
    • iPhone: notification sounds no longer pause your music.
    • Account badges count only what your Vault list shows.
    • Live chat opens at the newest message, and lives like Lot Radio show in Live Following.
    • Mac: the vault starts on its own only when Start Vault Automatically is on, and the window fits a 13-inch MacBook Air.

    Build 41 · iPhone, Mac and Android

    3.0.0

    Nostr Vault 3.0 connects your own devices directly over the mesh, with no server in between.

    • Your devices, connected. Turn on Mesh in Settings and add your devices by address or QR code. On Android you can add your Mac, your other phones or a mobile vault. See My Devices.
    • iPhone and Mac: the mesh engine runs inside the Mac app, so there is nothing else to install. The Mac serves your photos to your own devices, and your public photos to anyone else who asks. See your own photo server.
    • Notes and photos to your Mac. Add your Mac as Desktop vault · full history and each new note and photo goes to it over the mesh. Your notes and history also sync back from the Mac.
    • iPhone and Mac: Check desktop vault sync (now Sync now) ends on "Done" with counts, or says why it stopped.
    • Mobile vault. A spare phone can be your mobile vault. It gets your new posts and media and passes them on. See Mobile vault mode.
    • Photos over the mesh. Photos kept on your devices load over the mesh in the feed, the Vault and profiles.
    • Your address stays private. Once you add a device, only your devices learn this phone's address. Anyone else gets public photos only. On Android, that is up to the limit you set under Pause after. See privacy.
    • Android: if the mesh was on, it starts again with the relay when the phone restarts.

    Fixes

    • iPhone: the phone ran hot with the app open. The relay now gets memory sized to the phone.
    • iPhone and Mac: search no longer freezes while you type a common word.
    • Android: on some phones the app ran out of memory and closed when a long note full of links was in the feed.

    Posting a photo still needs a public media server.

    iPhone and Mac build 19 · Android build 20

    2.7.2 (first builds)

    Music, hashtags, highlights, and direct messages that reach every device you own. On Android, the app now matches the iPhone: same tabs, same wording, same feed, thread and Relay tab.

    iPhone and Mac, coming from 2.7.0 (build 15)? That was the last GitHub download. 2.7.1 and 2.7.2 brought a built-in Lightning wallet, Sign In With Clave, a Web of Trust switch and language filter for Global, and a security pass on fetched notes and zap receipts.

    Security (iPhone and Mac)

    • A mention could plant a fake link (iPhone). A profile name wasn't escaped before it went into the link that makes a mention tappable. A crafted display name could break out of that link and put an attacker's link elsewhere in the note, just by being mentioned. Names are now escaped.
    • A note could make your phone call out to hundreds of hosts. Every link in a note got its own preview card, and each card fetches its page. Previews now stop at 3 per note. The rest stay as text.
    • An early Follow tap could wipe your follow list. A timeout or error could count as your follow list having loaded, and a tap in that window replaced your follow list everywhere with a list of one. Now only a real, signed list (or every relay agreeing there is none) counts, and an early tap waits instead.
    • Setup accepted an npub that didn't match your key. A hand-edited npub could be saved next to a different nsec, and a one-character typo in an npub was accepted as a different person. The npub is now worked out from the nsec you paste, and a typed npub must pass its checksum.
    • Highlights could be forged or flooded. Every highlight is now signature-checked, length-capped, and refused if dated more than 10 minutes in the future.

    New

    • Music. Wavlake music that keeps playing in the background. iPhone and Mac: a music feed with Trending, Artists, Following and search; lock-screen, Control Center and Mac Now Playing controls; artist and album pages, an Up Next queue, shuffle and repeat, and AirPlay. A Wavlake link in a post shows a play card, and sharing a song mentions the artist's Nostr profile so they're notified. Android: shuffle, repeat, a queue and artist pages, and MP3s shared in posts play from a card.
    • Hashtags. Tap a hashtag in a post to open a live feed of everything carrying it. On iPhone and Mac you can show it from everyone or just people you follow. Android: you can also follow hashtags and get a feed of them, with a chip for each tag and suggestions to follow.
    • Articles and highlights. The article reader lets you like, comment, zap, highlight and share. Select a passage to highlight it. Other people's highlights show in the text. iPhone and Mac: the actions sit under the byline and at the end of the piece, a highlight can carry an optional thought, and a highlight count shows who highlighted. Android: you can comment on highlights, and profiles have Articles, diVines and Music tabs.
    • Post diVines, articles and recipes. Post from their own feeds. iPhone and Mac: the Post button writes what the feed in front of you shows. In diVines (formerly Reels) you can record up to 6 seconds or pick a video, trimmed to loop cleanly. In Articles and Recipes it opens a long-form editor.
    • GIFs from nostr.build. The GIF picker searches nostr.build, which licenses its GIFs for this use.
    • Text length. Choose in Settings how many lines of text Compact and Threaded views show.
    • Live streams. iPhone: a quoted stream, or a zap.stream, shosho or njump link, plays in the feed, with an ENDED pill once it's over. Android: streams play inside posts, pop out to a moving mini player, rejoin after a drop, and live chat takes pictures from your media servers.
    • iPhone and Mac: replies follow the threads spec. Answering an article, video, highlight or a repost of one now sends a NIP-22 comment, which other apps thread correctly. Replying to a plain note still sends a normal reply. Threads gain a "Quotes & highlights" section.
    • iPhone and Mac: image and link addresses are hidden from note text, with one preview card per link, and a domain-only card when a site has no preview.
    • iPad: Feed, Relay, Search and Profile show the list and the note side by side in portrait too.
    • Android: a Marketplace feed, a Shop tab on profiles, Sell a listing, and Message seller.
    • Android: posts in another language get a Translate button. Translation runs on your phone (ML Kit), and the text is not sent anywhere.
    • Android: see and vote on polls in the feed and in a note.
    • Android: profiles show their banner, and Edit Profile has a Banner URL field.
    • Android: tap to react, hold to pick a reaction, and a Thread Stats panel.
    • Android: media on a profile opens the full viewer, with video, audio and drag to close. The Media grid has Report and Block.
    • Android: the feed dashboard has Noise Filtering and Actions.
    • Android: pick the sound for notifications.
    • Android: select and copy text in a note.
    • Android: Search has filters and tappable links. People in your Web of Trust are listed right after the people you follow, in Search and in @mentions.
    • Android: every post has an Event Info panel with the relays it came from, its details and actions.
    • Android: the Relay tab gets Likes Given and Zaps Given, count sheets, search, a compact layout, Whitelisted, load more, and tap the tab to jump to the top.
    • Android: the Media tab can upload several files at once, paste, sort, show dates, and has a grid menu.
    • Android: a Mosaic media widget, widgets you can set up, avatars in the feed widget, and a choice of app icon.
    • Android: posts sent from Nostr Vault carry a small glowing badge.
    • Android: pair a signer with a nostrconnect:// link, and switch signer accounts without logging in again.

    Direct messages

    • One inbox on every device. iPhone and Mac: your copy of a sent message could land on relays no device reads messages from. There is now one message inbox list for every device, with your mobile or desktop vault first, and messages stranded by earlier builds are fetched back once. A private Mac address is never published. Android: one message list across all your devices, and messages you sent from another app are caught up once.
    • Alerts. No alert for your own sent messages. Tapping an alert opens the chat. iPhone and Mac: no alert for a chat you already have open, and the message box clears after sending. Android: notifications show the message; with notifications off, a banner shows inside the app; photos show in the conversation.
    • Android: message signatures are checked, and a forged sender is rejected.
    • Android: a new account can be messaged straight away, and messages arrive live.

    Changed and removed

    • iPhone and Mac: getyarn.io is removed from the GIF keyboard in every build. There is no permission to use it.
    • iPhone and Mac: the "Post as Comment" switch is gone. The reply type is now chosen automatically.
    • Android: the location is taken out of photos and videos before they upload.
    • Android: the Threaded view of Global and Popular loads the replies under each post, and bare reposts show in thread lines.
    • Android: the "Approve in your signer" banner is gone.
    • Android: Following, Global and the shield work the same way in every feed. Everyone is a crossed-out shield.
    • Android: notifications come from your Web of Trust, and zap notifications name the person who zapped you.
    • Android: replies to your own posts are kept even when they come from outside your network.
    • Android: the app asks to confirm before you delete or remove something.
    • Android: group chats (NIP-29) are removed.

    Fixes

    • Blocked people stay out of every feed. On iPhone and Mac, blocking didn't refresh the feed, and reposts, replies and thread context from a blocked person slipped through. They're now filtered everywhere the moment you block.
    • Photo posts no longer wait behind a signer request nobody answered. On iPhone and Mac this could hold a photo post for 90 seconds after returning to the app.
    • Follow taps made while your list is still loading are applied once it loads. On iPhone and Mac, "Contacts still loading" could stick forever.
    • Reposts show when the original note was written.
    • iPhone and Mac: the Zaps Given list fetches sent zaps from your feed relays again, survives a refresh, and shows the note you actually zapped.
    • iPhone: the feed ran hot while scrolling. There is less work on the main thread while notes stream in, and the feed picker menu no longer stutters.
    • iPhone: banners and the zap strike now draw above any open sheet.
    • iPhone and Mac: each remote signer keeps its own session, so switching accounts no longer reconnects from scratch, and your relay sign-in always goes to your own signer.
    • iPhone and Mac: your Media tab showed reposted media. Only your own uploads appear there now.
    • iPhone and Mac: in the Web of Trust, changing the depth rebuilds the graph, the graph saves at every depth, and a slow relay no longer drops lists already received.
    • iPhone and Mac: GIFs beside thread lines animate and fill the square instead of sitting still and letterboxed.
    • iPhone: Edit and Undo on the post countdown pill take taps again.
    • Android: a repost opens, zaps, reports and replies as the note it reposts.
    • Android: the Web of Trust list was read and saved wrongly, which emptied Global and Discovery. Fixed.
    • Android: Following reads the people you follow from their own relays and keeps bare reposts.
    • Android: big follow lists can be signed through a remote signer.
    • Android: saving your profile keeps your other fields, such as your banner.
    • Android: nprofile links open the right profile, link addresses are hidden behind one card per link, and a post is sent from the account that was active when you tapped Post.

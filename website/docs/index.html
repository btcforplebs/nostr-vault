    How Nostr Vault's mesh works: your phone or Mac serves your own photos over the FIPS mesh, with no public IP, domain or cloud. My Devices, kiosk mode, share pages and privacy.

    Connect your iPhone, Android phone, Mac, home vault and browser page over the FIPS mesh, by address or QR code, so they sync with each other.

    Sync with your Mac

    Send each new note and photo from your phone to Nostr Vault on your Mac over the mesh, get your history back from the Mac, and check that everything arrived.

    Turn a spare phone into an always-on home vault with kiosk mode: it keeps your new posts and media, passes your notes on to your relays, and serves your public photos.

    Get started

    Use Nostr Vault in a browser

    On this page

    • Sign in
    • Opening a shared post
    • Find your way around
    • Your data in this browser
    • Next

    The web app is Nostr Vault in a browser tab, with nothing to install. It's in beta. Open nostrvault.app/vault/ in any up-to-date browser, on a computer or a phone.

    Like the apps, it runs your own relay, here inside the browser. Your notes and messages are kept in this browser's storage.

    Sign in

    The web app never asks for your secret key. Sign in with something that holds the key for you:

    • A browser extension. If a Nostr signing extension is installed in this browser, Sign in with extension shows. Click it and approve in the extension.
    • A remote signer. Paste a bunker:// link from your signer app into Bunker link (NIP-46), click Connect bunker, and approve the connection in your signer. See Signers.
    • Read-only. Type your name, npub or name@domain and click Find me. You can read your feed but not post.

    Accounts you've used in this browser are listed at the top next time, so one click signs you back in.

    Opening a shared post

    A post shared from Nostr Vault opens in the web app, and anyone can read it without signing in. The top says Viewing as a guest. Click Sign in there to reply, like or zap. A guest's browser keeps nothing once the tab is closed.

    Find your way around

    The same places as the phone apps: Feed, Search, Profile, WoT and Vault. On a phone they're a tab bar at the bottom; in a wide window they're a sidebar, and a post opens next to the list. Settings is the gear on your profile.

    Your data in this browser

    • Settings → Backup & Restore → Download backup saves every event you signed that this browser holds, one per line (.jsonl). Messages and what others sent you aren't in it.
    • Restore puts a backup file back into this browser. Only events signed by you are taken, and nothing is published.
    • Under This browser's copy, Sign out and delete this browser's copy removes everything this browser holds. Your phone's vault is untouched.

    Clearing your browser's site data deletes the web app's copy too. Your posts are still on public relays and in your phone or Mac vault.

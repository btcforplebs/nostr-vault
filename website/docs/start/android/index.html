    Get started on Android

    Nostr Vault runs your own Nostr relay inside the app. Your posts, messages and media are kept on your phone, and copies go out to public relays so people can see them.

    Install

    Nostr Vault needs Android 8.0 or later. Get it from Zapstore, or download the APK from the latest release on GitHub and open it. Android asks you to allow installs from that app the first time.

    Pick a way in

    The first screen has two buttons. What's inside? under them lists what the app does.

    • Create an account if you're new to Nostr.
    • I already use Nostr if you have an account from another Nostr app. Your old notes come home to your phone.

    Where should your notes live?

    Every way in asks this first.

    • Built-in Relay (recommended): your own relay and media server, inside the app. Pick this unless you know you want the other one.
    • A Relay You Already Run: a relay app on this phone, such as Citrine, or your own relay elsewhere, such as Nostr Vault on your Mac. Fill in Relay URL, and Blossom URL (optional) for media. The built-in relay never starts, and setup skips importing your notes.

    You can change it later in Settings → Advanced.

    Create an account

    1. Welcome to Nostr. Tap Create My Account.
    2. Your Keys. The public key is your name on Nostr; share it freely. The secret key is your password; anyone who has it can post as you. Copy it into your password manager. Nobody can recover it for you. Tick I saved my secret key, then Continue.
    3. Protect Your Key. Choose a password of at least 8 characters. It locks your secret key on this phone, and nobody can reset it.
    4. Your Profile. Add a photo and a name, or tap Skip for Now.
    5. Welcome to Nostr! Tap Start Exploring, then follow the short guide that helps you find people. Five follows build your web of trust, which fills your feed and filters out spam.

    Bring the account you have

    1. Tap I already use Nostr, choose where your notes live, and the screen asks Who are you on Nostr?
    2. Paste your npub or name@domain (Read-only), your nsec (You can post, and you choose a password), an ncryptsec (Encrypted key · you can post), or a bunker:// link (Signer link · you can post). Scan your key or signer link reads a QR code.
    3. Or use a signer, so your secret key never enters Nostr Vault:
      • Use Amber shows when Amber is installed on this phone.
      • Use a signer app then Sign in with a signer app opens Amber or another signer on this phone, or shows a code to scan with a signer on another device.
      See Signers.
    4. Checking your relays. Tap Start import.
    5. Bringing your notes home. Wait for the import, or tap Keep it running in the background and start using the app.

    Who told you about Nostr Vault?

    If you create an account, or bring one you can post with, setup asks Who told you about Nostr Vault? It comes after Your Profile, or after Checking your relays. Read-only accounts skip it.

    1. Tap Scan their profile code, or paste their profile link or npub. Their link comes from Share profile; see Share your profile. If you opened their link before setting up, they're already filled in.
    2. Check their profile card, and follow them. Not them? Try another goes back.
    3. Up to 10 people they follow come next, the most followed first, all ticked. Untick anyone you don't want, and follow the rest.

    Nobody told you? Tap Skip, nobody told me.

    Find your way around

    The tab bar along the bottom has Feed, Search, Profile in the middle, WoT (your web of trust) and Vault (everything your relay keeps, and your messages). The gear on your Profile opens Settings.

    Replay the app's short tours from Settings → Help → Tutorials.

What it is

Nostr has no central moderator. Instead, Nostr Vault trusts who you trust. Your web has three rings:

  • Following: the people you follow.
  • Vouched: people that 10 or more of your follows follow.
  • Further out: people your follows follow, but fewer than 10 of them.

Your vault rebuilds the web on its own, once a day unless you change Refresh Trust List. If you follow very few people, it borrows from your follows' follows so a new account isn't empty.

New in 3.2.2

One web per account

With more than one account on a device, each one now has its own web, built from its own follows. Before 3.2.2 the app pooled every account's follows into one web, so someone followed by one of your follows and two of another account's follows counted as vouched for you. To you they were three hops out.

  • The open account sees its own web in feeds, the Vault list, notifications and the WoT globe: you, your follows, and people enough of your own follows follow.
  • Your other accounts get their alerts, their account badge and their Vault lines judged by their own web too.
  • The vault's inbox still uses one list for the device, pooled from every account, so a mention for any account can land. What you're shown from it is filtered by the open account's web.

Until the open account's follow list has loaded, and for a new account with fewer follows than Minimum Followers, the app shows the shared web instead.

The WoT tab

The globe

Open the WoT tab. You're in the middle. Your follows sit on the first ring, Vouched people on the next, and everyone else is a faint outer shell. Each person always sits in the same spot, because their place comes from their key.

  • Layers. The menu at the top left picks Everyone, Following, Vouched or Further out, each with a count. Vouched shows up once your vault has counted your web.
  • Filters. Talk with you keeps people you've replied to, reposted, liked or zapped, or who did that to you. Follow you back keeps follows who follow you too.
  • Find someone. Tap the magnifier and type a name. Your follows come first, then your web, then everyone else.

Why someone is here

Trust paths and their web

Tap a face to open their card. The line under their name says how they reach you, for example "You follow them · 1 hop" or "Followed by Ann, Bo + more you follow · 2 hops". If no one you follow follows them, it says "Not in your web".

From the card you can follow or unfollow, open their Profile, or tap See their web to put them in the middle and see the globe from their side. A globe opened from a profile or a post has Open in WoT instead, which takes you to the WoT tab.

A post's Event Info shows the same trust path for its author.

The numbers

My Web and rebuilding

Tap My Web for the whole picture:

  • New in your web: faces of people who joined your web since you last looked. Tap one to open their card on the globe. The first time you open it on a device, it only notes who's already there.
  • Who you can see: how many people are in your whole web, how many you follow, how many are Vouched and how many are further out. Tap a row to show that layer on the globe.
  • With you: who talks with you and who follows you back.
  • Upkeep: when the web was last rebuilt, and Rebuild your web. On iPhone and Android, Plebs vs Zombies opens a site that helps you clean up your follows.

Just followed a lot of people? Tap Rebuild your web (in My Web, or at the bottom of the layer menu). The globe shows each step: reading your follow list, reading your follows' lists, counting. A full rebuild can take a few minutes. If it fails or the vault isn't running, the globe keeps your last saved web.

Where it's used

What your web decides

  • Who can reach your vault. Only people in your web can post to your vault's inbox. Strangers can still reply to or quote your own posts.
  • Notifications. Replies, likes, reposts and mentions notify you only from people in your web. Zaps and follows always come through. In the Vault list, likes and reposts from outside your web fold into one "N from outside your web" line; tap it to see them under Outside your web. They don't add to the account's unread count. A message alerts if it's from your web or someone you've written to; others wait under Requests. A new follower outside your web is folded into "N new followers".
  • Feeds. Global, Media and the other discovery feeds show only your web unless you switch them to Everyone. Everyone unlocks once you follow 5 people. While it's on, a banner at the top says so and takes you back to your web in one tap. See Feeds.
  • Search. Your follows and your web rank above everyone else. People and notes from outside your web fold under one line at the end.
  • Mesh photos. Photos load over the mesh only from people you follow, not from your wider web.

Settings

Who Can Reach You

On iPhone and Android, open Settings → Your Vault → Who Can Reach You.

  • Follow Distance is how far out your web reaches. It counts you as 1: at 2 it's you and your follows, at 3 (the default) it adds the people they follow. That outer ring is what a trust card calls "2 hops". It never goes further, so nobody three hops out is in your web.
  • Minimum Followers is how many of your follows must follow someone in that outer ring before they're in. The default is 3. Your own follows are always in. It's counted from the open account's follows.
  • Refresh Trust List is how often the web is rebuilt.

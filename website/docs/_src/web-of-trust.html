What it is

Nostr has no central moderator. Instead, Nostr Vault trusts who you trust. Your web has three rings:

  • Following: the people you follow.
  • Vouched: people that 10 or more of your follows follow.
  • Further out: people your follows follow, but fewer than 10 of them.

Your vault rebuilds the web on its own, once a day unless you change Refresh Trust List. If you follow very few people, it borrows from your follows' follows so a new account isn't empty.

New in 3.2.2

One web per account

With more than one account on a device, each one now has its own web, built from its own follows. Before 3.2.2 the app pooled every account's follows into one web, so someone followed by one of your follows and two of another account's follows counted as vouched for you. To you they were three hops out.

  • The open account sees its own web in feeds, the Vault list, notifications and the WoT globe: you, your follows, and people enough of your own follows follow.
  • Your other accounts get their alerts, their account badge and their Vault lines judged by their own web too.
  • The vault's inbox still uses one list for the device, pooled from every account, so a mention for any account can land. What you're shown from it is filtered by the open account's web.

Until the open account's follow list has loaded, and for a new account with fewer follows than Minimum Followers, the app shows the shared web instead.

The WoT tab

The globe

Open the WoT tab. You're in the middle. Your follows sit on the first ring, Vouched people on the next, and everyone else is a faint outer shell. Each person always sits in the same spot, because their place comes from their key.

  • Layers. The menu at the top left picks Everyone, Following or Vouched, each with a count, and on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android Hop 3, Hop 4 and Hop 5 (the far rings). Vouched shows up once your vault has counted your web. The web app has Further out in place of the hops.
  • Filters. Talk with you keeps people you've replied to, reposted, liked or zapped, or who did that to you. Follow you back keeps follows who follow you too.
  • Find someone. Tap the magnifier and type a name. Your follows come first, then your web, then everyone else.
  • Live stars. New posts from your web fly in as stars while you watch. Your follows and the rest of your web fly as plain stars; from further out, only zaps fly. Nobody you blocked or muted ever flies. My Web has three switches for it: Live stars, Friends of friends and Likes, reposts and zaps (on the Mac, the antenna menu in the WoT bar). (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.)

The far rings: Hop 3, Hop 4 and Hop 5

Past your web are faint rings for the people three, four and five hops out. Your phone can't rank that many people itself, so a desktop Vault (a Mac or a Linux box) ranks them and sends the scores to your phone over the mesh. A Mac does this for itself.

  • Picking a hop. Pick Hop 3, Hop 4 or Hop 5 in the layer menu and the globe zooms out to that ring: your inner globe shrinks to the middle and the ring fills the screen. Each ring shows its best-ranked people, up to 300, labelled Found by your desktop Vault. With more than one desktop paired, My Web names the one that ranked them.
  • Greyed hops. Until a desktop Vault's scores are on this device, the hops are greyed with a lock and Needs a desktop Vault. Tapping one says A desktop Vault unlocks the far rings. With no desktop Vault, a card offers Pair a desktop Vault to see distant connections; Not now puts it away, and Settings → Feed → Discovery Reach brings it back. A paired Vault that hasn't sent its scores yet shows faint rings with nobody on them.
  • The haze. Zoomed out to Hop 3 or Hop 4, the people ranked after the ring's top 300 show as a faint haze of dots: the best near the middle, the lowest drifting out past the ring. Each dot keeps its place. Haze dots can't be tapped.

The globe always draws on a dark sky, also in Light mode.

Why someone is here

Trust paths and their web

Tap a face to open their card. The line under their name says how they reach you, for example "You follow them · 1 hop" or "Followed by Ann, Bo + more you follow · 2 hops". If no one you follow follows them, it says "Not in your web".

From the card you can follow or unfollow, open their Profile, or tap See their web to put them in the middle and see the globe from their side. A globe opened from a profile or a post has Open in WoT instead, which takes you to the WoT tab.

A post's Event Info shows the same trust path for its author.

The numbers

My Web and rebuilding

Tap My Web for the whole picture:

  • New in your web: faces of people who joined your web since you last looked. Tap one to open their card on the globe. The first time you open it on a device, it only notes who's already there.
  • Who you can see: how many people are in your whole web, how many you follow, how many are Vouched and how many are further out. Tap a row to show that layer on the globe.
  • With you: who talks with you and who follows you back.
  • Upkeep: when the web was last rebuilt, and Rebuild your web. On iPhone and Android, Plebs vs Zombies opens a site that helps you clean up your follows.

Just followed a lot of people? Tap Rebuild your web (in My Web, or at the bottom of the layer menu). The globe shows each step: reading your follow list, reading your follows' lists, counting. A full rebuild can take a few minutes. If it fails or the vault isn't running, the globe keeps your last saved web.

Where it's used

What your web decides

  • Who can reach your vault. Only people in your web can post to your vault's inbox. Strangers can still reply to or quote your own posts.
  • Notifications. Replies, likes, reposts and mentions notify you only from people in your web. In the Vault list, likes and reposts from outside your web fold into one "N from outside your web" line; tap it to see them under Outside your web. They don't add to the account's unread count. A message alerts if it's from your web or someone you've written to; others wait under Requests.
  • Zaps. On iPhone, iPad and Mac a zap is judged by who paid. From your web you see the whole zap. From outside it, you see the amount without its comment, and it doesn't alert you (it's still in the Vault's Zaps). From someone you blocked, nothing.
  • Followers. Your Followers list shows the followers in your web. The rest are under ••• → Outside your web, and only followers in your web count toward new-follower alerts and the red dot.
  • Feeds, replies and live chat. Since 3.2.3 every feed goes through one gate on iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web: no stranger reaches a feed, not even by tagging you, and blocked and muted people are left out by the same rule. A post's replies and quotes and a live stream's chat use it too. Global and the other discovery feeds still show everyone when you switch them to Everyone (that unlocks once you follow 5 people). See Feeds.
  • Search. Your follows and your web rank above everyone else. People and notes from outside your web fold under one line at the end.
  • Mesh photos. Photos load over the mesh only from people you follow, not from your wider web.

New in 3.2.3

Block, private block and nuclear unfollow

A profile's ••• menu has three ways to keep someone out of your web. They're on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and the web.

  • Block hides them everywhere. Your block list is public, so others can see who you blocked.
  • Private block hides them the same way, but writes them only into the encrypted part of your block list. Nobody else can see it. It needs a key or signer that can encrypt.
  • Nuclear unfollow is for someone who got into your web through your follows. The app finds everyone you follow who follows them and lights them on the globe. Tap Show list to see them, all ticked; untick anyone you want to keep. It always asks first, and asks a second time at 25 people or more. Then it saves a backup of your follow list and publishes one new list without the people you ticked. Undo puts the backup back. It doesn't block or mute anyone.

Blocked people, public or private, are taken out of your web, so they don't reach feeds, replies, zaps or the globe. Settings → Blocked lists them in two parts, Public and Private, and Block privately there blocks someone new privately.

Settings

Who Can Reach You

On iPhone and Android, open Settings → Your Vault → Who Can Reach You.

  • Follow Distance is how far out your web reaches. At 1 hop it's you and your follows, at 2 hops (the default) it adds the people they follow, the same "2 hops" a trust card shows. It never goes further, so nobody three hops out is in your web.
  • Minimum Followers is how many of your follows must follow someone in that outer ring before they're in. The default is 3. Your own follows are always in. It's counted from the open account's follows.
  • Refresh Trust List is how often the web is rebuilt.

  • The web app has a WoT tab with the Globe, the Everyone, Following, Vouched and Further out layers, Find someone, Follow you back, trust cards with See their web, and My Web with Rebuild your web.

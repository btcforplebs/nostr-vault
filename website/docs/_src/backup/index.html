Notes and media

Backup & Restore

Open Settings → Backup & Restore (in the Your Vault group on iPhone, iPad and Android; on the Mac, Settings → Backups → Backup & Restore). It has two parts, and each one is its own file.

  • Notes. Export Notes (on the Mac, Save JSONL Backup) saves everything in your vault as a .zip: your notes, private notes, messages (still encrypted), the notes that mention you and your feed cache. Import Notes puts a notes backup back.
  • Media. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, save your photos and videos with Back up media on the Vault dashboard, under Keep it full; Import Media here puts a media backup back. On Android this page has both Export Media and Import Media.

On iPhone the file goes to the share sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it to your Mac. On the Mac and Android you pick where to save it.

A restore adds to what's already in the vault. Anything it already has is skipped, and nothing is deleted. The vault pauses for a moment while notes are saved or restored. If any note or file fails to restore, it says so instead of reporting success, and a file that isn't a media backup is turned down.

Your keys are not in the backup. A backup holds notes and media only. Keep your key (or your signer) safe on its own; see Accounts & Keys in the app.

Your wallet connection and signer links aren't in your phone's own device backup either: they stay in the phone's secure storage, on that phone only. On iPhone, iPad and Mac your message cache is left out of the device backup too. On a new phone, connect your wallet and signer again.

Start the vault before you back up media. The Vault dashboard has both under Keep it full: Export notes and Back up media.

Your follow list

Following Backup

A follow list on Nostr is one list that gets replaced every time it changes. A buggy app can wipe it. Nostr Vault keeps a snapshot each time your list changes, up to 50, on your device.

  1. Open Settings → Following Backup.
  2. Pick a snapshot. It shows who you no longer follow and who was added since. You can re-follow one person, or tap Restore This List to put the whole list back.
  3. Confirm. The restored list replaces your current one and is published to your relays.

If your list was wiped somewhere else, Scan Relays looks for older copies of it on your relays.

Every list you keep

List backups

Your vault also keeps the old versions of your other lists: mutes, bookmarks, relays, follow sets and more. Open List backups from the Vault dashboard or Settings (on the web, Recover a List). Each version says which app saved it and what changed. If a list suddenly shrinks, a banner warns you, and Merge puts the old entries back alongside the new ones, so nothing you added since is lost.

Look up and back up my lists (on by default, on the Vault dashboard; on Android also while you set up) goes further back: it asks history relays for older copies of your lists, the way the Lazarus spec finds them, and saves them in your list history. A follow list another app emptied months ago can still come back. It runs from the Vault's Still to bring in card, as Old versions of your lists.

Undo a delete

Recently Deleted

Posts you delete on a device stay in Settings → Recently Deleted on that device for 30 days.

  • Un-delete posts it again as a new post. Likes and replies on the old post don't come back.
  • Delete Forever removes the copy now.

Profiles, lists and articles aren't kept here. Recently Deleted is in the apps and the web app.

In the browser

Backups on the web

The web app keeps its vault in your browser, so back it up if it holds anything you don't have elsewhere.

  • Settings → Backup & Restore: Export notes saves your notes, private notes, messages, the notes that mention you and feed cache as the same .zip the phone apps make, so either one restores the other. Then tap Save backup file to keep it. Photos and videos aren't in it.
  • Vault Dashboard → Keep it full → Back up media saves this browser's photos and videos as a .zip. The web app can't import media.
  • Import notes reads that .zip back. It also reads a .jsonl backup from an older web app. Nothing is published either way.

On a Mac, Nostr Vault lives in the menu bar and runs your own Nostr relay. With Start Vault Automatically on, the relay starts when the app opens; otherwise click Start in the menu bar panel. Leave it running and your Mac becomes the always-on home for your notes and media.

Install

Nostr Vault needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Get it from the Mac App Store, the same listing as on iPhone and iPad.

The first time it opens, a Welcome to Nostr Vault window says Nostr Vault lives in your menu bar. Click Get Started. Setup opens next.

Pick a way in

  • Create an account if you're new to Nostr.
  • Bring an account to bring your account, your notes and your media.
  • Just look around to read with your public key only.

Create an account

The same steps as on the iPhone: Create My Account, save your secret key on Your Keys and tick I saved my secret key, choose a password on Protect Your Key, then a photo and name on Your Profile (or Skip for Now), the relays your posts go to on Choose where your posts go (the list starts empty; take our suggestions or add your own), who told you, and Start Exploring. The details are on Get started on iPhone.

About the password on Protect Your Key: This password locks the secret key you just saved. It stays on this device only and is never sent to us or any server. We cannot recover or reset it. If you forget it, the secret key you saved is your only way back in.

Bring the account you have

  1. Enter Your Keys. Pick how you sign:
    • Private Key: paste your nsec and choose a password. The key is encrypted on this Mac.
    • Remote Signer: Connect with Clave on your iPhone shows a code to scan with Clave. Or paste a bunker link from any signer app. Your key stays in the signer.
    No account yet? New to Nostr? Generate Keys makes one here. A key made this way skips the servers step and the profile, so set your relays later in Settings → Relays.
  2. Your Vault. Your vault runs on this Mac either way. A Relay Domain (optional) makes it reachable from your other devices. Leave it blank for local only, or tap Skip — configure later.
  3. New to Nostr Vault? Yes, bring my notes from my relays goes on to the import. Copy from my other vault links this Mac to your other device with two scans and copies your vault across over the mesh, the same way as on the iPhone. If it can't be reached, your notes come from your relays. A backup .zip is restored later from the Vault tab (Restore from a backup).
  4. Bring Your Notes Home. Copies your notes from public relays to this Mac, and also reads the write relays you published yourself (it says Also reading from your servers). Tap Import Notes, or Skip — I'll start fresh.
  5. Mirror Your Media. Copies your photos and videos from a media server, or from a backup, to this Mac. Tap Sync Media or Skip Media.
  6. Lightning (NWC). Paste a Nostr Wallet Connect link to send zaps, or Skip — set up later.
  7. You're All Set. Click Launch Nostr Vault.

Who told you about Nostr Vault?

Setup for a new account asks who told you about Nostr Vault, just before the last screen. Paste their profile link or npub, check their profile card and follow them, then pick from up to 10 people they follow (all ticked to start). Skip, nobody told me leaves it out. On a Mac a profile link can't fill this in for you, so paste it. Details on Get started on iPhone.

Just look around

Enter Your Public Key (your npub or name@domain), answer New to Nostr Vault?, import your notes if you like, and Ready to Browse opens the app. Re-run setup later to post, DM and zap.

Find your way around

The menu bar panel. Click the Nostr Vault icon in the menu bar.

  • The top line says whether your vault is running, for how long, and how many devices are connected. If it isn't running it says Phones and the web can't reach it now.
  • Tiles show what's Stored, your mesh, and the Outbox when posts are waiting to go out.
  • Open Nostr Vault opens the full window. New Note starts a post (⌘N). Start or Restart starts your relay.
  • At the bottom: your account picture to switch accounts, the gear (Settings (⌘,)), and the power button (Quit Nostr Vault), which asks first, because quitting stops your vault.

The window. A slim rail down the left has Feed, Search, Profile, WoT, Vault, Media and Relay, with Settings at the bottom; ⌘1 to ⌘7 jump down the rail. A note you open shows in a Thread column beside the list. With no note open, Your space sits on the right.

Settings has six pages: Account, Your Vault, Relays & Media, Backups, Reading and Help. Each page holds several sections, with a bar at the top to jump to one.

To keep your vault running, open Settings → Your Vault; it starts with Startup. Turn on Launch at Login and Start Vault Automatically.

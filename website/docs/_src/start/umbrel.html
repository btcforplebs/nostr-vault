umbrelOS runs Nostr Vault as an app on your Umbrel: the same box as Nostr Vault for Linux, with umbrelOS doing the installs and updates. It comes from our own community app store, so you add the store once and install from it.

Add the store and install

  1. On your Umbrel, open App Store, then the ⋯ menu at the top right, then Community App Stores.
  2. Paste https://github.com/btcforplebs/umbrel-app-store and tap Add.
  3. Open Nostr Vault from the new store and tap Install. It runs on Umbrel Home, Raspberry Pi and other x86 or arm64 Umbrels.

Claim the box

A new box serves only a setup page until its owner claims it.

  1. On the app's page in umbrelOS, look under Default credentials. The password there is the box's setup code. It is also in the app's settings.
  2. Open the app. Paste your account's npub (never your nsec: the box never holds your private key) and the setup code, and tap Claim this box.
  3. Sign in on the box's page with the same account (a signer extension or a bunker).
  • The first npub with the right code owns the box, and the setup page is gone after that. Only that account can manage the box.
  • Too many wrong codes in a minute and the box waits before it accepts more.

Pair your phone

Pair from the box's page, as for any Linux box: Settings → This Box → My Devices → Add a device, scan the code with Nostr Vault on your phone, and check that both show the same four words. The steps are in A Linux box in My Devices.

  • At home your phone links to the box over your network. Away from home it reaches the box over the mesh, with no router ports opened.
  • The app uses host networking so the mesh can offer your phone the Umbrel's real network address.

Updates

umbrelOS offers each new version under Updates in the App Store. The box's own Update button says the same. Your data, owner and paired devices stay.

Backups and restore

  • umbrelOS backups copy the whole vault except the box's mesh key (with its old npub and mesh status).
  • After a restore the box makes a new mesh key, so it has a new npub. Your account, notes and media are all there, but your phone still knows the old box: on the phone, remove the old box under My Devices (Remove a device) and pair the box again.

Where the data is

The vault lives in the app's data folder on your Umbrel (~/umbrel/app-data/nostrvault-nostr-vault/data). Uninstalling the app deletes it, so back up first. The box's Move vault to… is turned off here: umbrelOS owns the disk.

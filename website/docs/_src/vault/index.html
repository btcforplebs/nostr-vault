The basics

What your vault is

Most Nostr apps only read and write to other people's relays. Nostr Vault also runs one on your device. Your vault is a Nostr relay and media server of your own, and it keeps a copy of:

  • Your own notes, everything you post, like, zap and save.
  • Notes that mention you, from people you trust (see Who Can Reach You).
  • Your direct messages, still encrypted.
  • Recent notes from people you follow, so your feed loads fast. Older ones are cleared out over time.
  • Your media, in its own media server.
  • The notes and media of your other accounts on this device, each in its own space (see Every account on this device).

The vault's address (wss://127.0.0.1:… on a phone) only works on that device. Other apps and people can't connect to it directly. What reaches it over the mesh or the web is set out in Who Can Reach You. Your notes still go to your normal relays too. The vault is the copy you own.

To see it explained in the app, open Settings → Help → Tutorials and play Your Vault at Work or How Your Vault Works.

More than one account

Every account on this device

The first account you set up is the owner: the vault is theirs. Accounts you add in Settings → Accounts & Keys share the same vault, in two different ways.

Space in the vaultReads the private side
The ownerYes, always, even with only an npubYes
An account with a key or signer on this deviceYesYes
A watch-only account (npub only)Yes on iPhone, iPad and Mac. No on AndroidNo
  • Space means the vault keeps that account's own notes and uploads, and shares them like yours.
  • The private side is your private notes, your old list versions, the full list of your media, and media you never posted. Only the owner and accounts that can sign on this device read it, by signing in with their own key.
  • A watch-only npub is often someone else's account. So it never reads the private side, and its notes can't be written into your private notes either. Only accounts that can read the private side can add to it.
  • Removing an account's key or signer keeps its space but stops its private reads. Removing the account takes away both.

After a copy from another device, iPhone, iPad and Mac ask Add your other accounts? The accounts you tick are added watch-only, each with its own space.

iPhone and Android

The Vault tab

The Vault tab opens on Vault, a list of everything new. Tap the Vault button at the bottom right (or pick Vault Dashboard at the end of the mode menu) to see how your vault is doing. At the top it says Your vault is running, starting, paused or needs a restart, with how long it's been up. If it stops, a button lets you start it again so it catches up on new notes and messages. The menu on that card has Copy Address, Restart, Stop and Start.

Below that:

  • Is it safe?: where your key is (on this device, in a signer app, or none), then three checks: Follow list saved, List backups and Backup file, with a button to make a backup file.
  • Who can reach you: how many people can send to your inbox, and how many people you've blocked.
  • In your vault: how many events it holds and how much space notes, media and cache use. The cache is other people's media kept for quick loading, and you can clear it.
  • Keep it full: Import notes, import media, back up media and export notes.
  • Vault settings: shortcuts to Relays, Who Can Reach You, Media Servers, Desktop vault sync and Start automatically.
  • Then relay activity, and Advanced with relay details and media servers.

On iPhone and Android this screen is called Vault Dashboard.

With more than one account on the device, chips over the Vault list switch between All and each account. Each chip shows that account's new count, and each line is marked with its account.

Settings

Vault settings

Everything about the vault is grouped under Settings → Your Vault. The card at the top of Settings shows whether it's running, with a Start Vault button if it isn't.

  • Start Vault Automatically: start the vault when the app opens. Leave it on.
  • Your Devices: the Macs, Linux boxes and phones you linked. Your media syncs to all of them (see The mesh).
  • Who Can Reach You: who may send to your inbox and messages. More.
  • Import Notes: fill the vault with your past notes. See below.
  • Backup & Restore: save or restore your vault as a file. More.

Bring your history

Import your past notes

A new vault starts empty. Import Notes pulls your old notes, and notes where you are tagged, from your other relays into the vault, starting at a date you pick.

  1. Open Settings → Your Vault → Import Notes and set the Start Date.
  2. Run it from the Vault Dashboard: Keep it full → Import notes. On Android you can also tap Start Import on the Import Notes page.

It reads from the relays that have Import switched on in Settings → Relays. Notes that tag you are kept only if they come from people you trust, or if they reply to or quote your own posts.

Phone, Mac and web

Where the vault runsWhat's different
iPhoneInside the appVault tab with the full dashboard.
AndroidInside the appSame as iPhone.
MacInside the menu bar appA simpler Relay Dashboard. Your devices reach it over the mesh with no setup; under Settings → Your Vault, Public on the web is the advanced last step: Make this vault public on the web lists your own domain in your relay list and DM inbox, and shows the domain, port and tunnel settings. Startup has Launch at Login.
WebIn your browser's storageA Vault Dashboard like the phone's, without Start/Stop, Import media or Desktop vault sync. Import with Keep it full → Import notes or Import from my relays in Settings. While the tab is open, files your devices send to this browser's vault show up in Media as they land, marked New. Signing out deletes this browser's copy; your phone's vault is untouched.

A vault on the web is a copy, not a backup. It lives in that browser. Use Sign out and delete this browser's copy on a shared computer, and keep real backups with Backup & Restore.

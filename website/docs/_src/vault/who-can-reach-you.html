The basics

What it controls

Your vault has an inbox, where notes that mention you land, and a chat store for your direct messages. Anyone on Nostr can try to send to them. Who Can Reach You keeps out everyone who isn't close to you in your follows. This is your Web of Trust, and it's the vault's spam filter.

  • Mentions and messages from people outside your trust list are turned away.
  • Replies and quotes of your own posts still get in, from anyone.
  • Your messages can only be read by people on the list who sign in.
  • Your own notes aren't affected. You can always post.

Open it from Settings → Your Vault → Who Can Reach You, or tap Who can reach you on the Vault Dashboard (Vault tab → Vault button), which shows how many people can reach your inbox right now.

The settings

Follow Distance, Minimum Followers, Refresh Trust List

Follow Distance

How far from you someone can be and still reach you. Lower is more private. A hop is one follow away from you, and you are never a hop, so the setting reads the same as a trust card.

Follow DistanceWho can reach youOn a trust card
Only youOnly you and your other accounts on this device.–
1 hopAlso the people you follow.1 hop
2 hops (default)Also the people they follow, if enough of your follows follow them (see Minimum Followers).2 hops

The control stops at 2 hops. An older, higher setting shows as 2 hops, which is how the relay always ran it. Nobody three hops out (a friend of a friend of a friend) gets into your inbox at any setting, unless they reply to or quote your own posts.

The Vault Dashboard counts the same way: at the default its line reads "People within 2 hops of you", meaning your follows and their follows. A trust card calls that same friend of a friend "2 hops".

Minimum Followers

At 2 hops, how many of the people you follow must also follow someone before they can reach you. The default is 3: a friend of a friend gets in once 3 of your follows follow them. Raise it to keep out more strangers, lower it to let more friends of friends in. 0 and 1 both mean one follow is enough. It has no effect at Only you or 1 hop, and your own follows always count, whatever it's set to.

A new account that follows fewer people than Minimum Followers would let almost nobody in, so the vault widens it to its follows' follows until the account follows more.

With more than one account

Since 3.3.0 each account has its own Follow Distance and Minimum Followers, and its inbox only lets in people from that account's own follows. Switch account to see or change its values. Your own accounts on the device can always reach each other. Refresh Trust List is one setting for the whole device. See Follow Distance.

Refresh Trust List

How often the vault rebuilds the list from your follows: Every hour, Every 12 hours, Every day (default) or Every week. Someone you just followed can reach you after the next rebuild.

Your devices are the ones in My Devices. Your other accounts on this device have their own space in the vault, and only those that can sign here read private notes and messages: see Every account on this device.

Where to find it

Phone, Mac and web

  • iPhone and Mac have the same screen. On the Mac it's in the Settings sidebar.
  • Android has the same settings, and restarts the vault for you when you change one.
  • The web has no Follow Distance. Its trust list is always 2 hops, as its Vault Dashboard says. Settings → Who Can Reach You has Minimum Followers and Refresh Trust List, kept in that browser. Its Who Can Reach You settings row opens the browser vault's mesh panel, where Who can reach this vault is a list of your own vaults' mesh addresses.

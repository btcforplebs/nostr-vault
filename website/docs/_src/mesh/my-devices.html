My Devices is the list of your own phones, Macs, home vault and browser page that reach each other over the mesh. They don't need a public IP or a domain: the mesh finds each device by its key, at home or on mobile data.

Devices on the list sync with each other: your notes, your photos and your history. Anyone not on the list can only read the photos in your public posts.

Turn the mesh on

On each device, open Settings → Mesh → FIPS Mesh and switch on Mesh. Under This Device you'll see its Mesh address, a long key starting with npub. That's how your other devices find this one.

Add a device

  1. On the device you want to add, open FIPS Mesh and, under This Device, tap Copy, or Show QR to scan it from the other device.
  2. On the other device, tap Add a device by address. Paste the address, or tap Scan its QR code.
  3. Under This Is, pick what it is:
    • Vault on Mac: Nostr Vault on your Mac. Your new notes and photos go to it, and your history syncs back. See Sync with your Mac.
    • Phone: another phone of yours.
    • Home vault: a spare phone in kiosk mode that keeps your posts. See Home vault and kiosk mode.
    • Browser page: a Nostr Vault page in a browser that keeps a copy of your notes.
  4. Give it a name if you like, and tap Add.
  5. Do it in both directions. Add the Mac on each phone, and each phone on the Mac. A device only lets in the devices on its own list.

You're done when both devices show each other as In reach under My Devices. It can take a moment after the mesh turns on.

The list

  • Each device shows In reach or Not in reach. A Mac is in reach while Nostr Vault is open on it; a phone while the app is open or in kiosk mode.
  • Tap a device to rename it, copy its address, or remove it. On Android, What It Gets chooses whether it gets Send new notes and Send new media.
  • Once a device is on the list, only the devices on the list learn this phone's address. Anyone else on the mesh gets public photos only.

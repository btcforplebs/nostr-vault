My Devices is the list of your own phones, iPads, Macs, home vault and browser vault that reach each other over the mesh. They don't need a public IP or a domain: the mesh finds each device by its key, at home on Wi-Fi or out on mobile data.

Devices on the list share your posts and media with each other. Anyone not on the list can only read the photos in your public posts.

Find Mesh settings

  • iPhone and iPad: the Mesh button at the top of the Profile tab, next to Settings. Or hold your profile picture in the tab bar and pick Mesh settings.
  • Android: hold your account picture and pick Mesh settings, or Settings → Mesh.
  • Mac: Mesh settings under Advanced on the Vault dashboard.
  • Everywhere, the Vault dashboard has a Your devices card with Mesh settings and Add.

Switch on Mesh. This Device shows this device's Mesh address, a long key starting with npub.

Link a device

Two devices link with two scans: one shows a code, the other scans it, then they swap. Both end up on each other's list, so you only do it once.

  1. On both devices, turn the mesh on. On one of them tap Link a device under This Device, or Add under Your devices.
  2. iPhone, iPad and Mac: tap Show my code on one device and Scan a code on the other. Android opens on its own code; tap Scan its code to scan the other one first, or Show this phone's code instead to go back. Web: the Your devices card has Show my code and Scan a code.
  3. The first device scans the second one's screen back. Each code lasts a few minutes.
  4. Check the words. Both devices show the same four words. If they match, tap They match (on Android, Do these words match?; tap They don't match if they don't). A browser with no camera links by the words alone.
  5. Confirm with Add (Android: Add it). The screen says Linked ✓ and the device's name. Tap Done.

No camera? On iPhone, iPad and Mac every scan step has Paste instead, and every code also shows as text with Copy. A Linux box or the browser relay page has no code, only a mesh address: paste that under Paste instead. To add this device to one of those, copy its address from Mesh → Advanced (Copy mesh address on the Mac, This Device's Address on Android).

Something went wrong? Start again shows a fresh code.

You're done when the other device shows a green dot under Your devices. It can take a moment once both are on the mesh.

Copy your vault to a new device

Setting up a new phone, iPad or Mac? Choose Copy from my other vault when setup asks New to Nostr Vault? The two devices link with two scans as above, then the new one copies the whole vault across over the mesh: your notes, photos and videos, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. Every account on the old device that can sign is offered too. Keep Nostr Vault open on the old device while it copies. If it can't be reached, the new one tries again every 15 seconds for five minutes, then offers Try again. The steps are on iPhone and Android. The web app links the same way, but takes your notes from your relays.

The list

  • Under My Devices, a phone shows each device's role and whether it is connected now, or Not in reach. Android shows In reach or when it was last seen. A Mac is in reach while Nostr Vault is open on it; a phone while the app is open or in Home vault mode.
  • A linked Mac is your Home vault · full history: your new notes and photos go to it, and your history syncs back. See Home vault sync. Your iPhone and Android phones also send new notes and media to every linked phone.
  • Tap a device to rename it, copy its address, or remove it (Remove from My Devices on iPhone). On Android, What It Gets chooses whether it gets Send new notes and Send new media.
  • Once a device is on the list, only the devices on the list learn this phone's address. Anyone else on the mesh gets public photos only.

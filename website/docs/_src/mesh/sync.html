With your Mac on your phone's list as Desktop vault · full history, each new note and photo you post on the phone also goes to the Mac over the mesh. Your notes and history sync back from the Mac to the phone. The Mac becomes the second, always-on copy of your vault.

Set it up

  1. On the phone, open Settings → Your Devices and tap Connect a computer or phone; on the Mac, open Settings → Your Vault and tap Connect a phone. Do the two scans (see Link a device). This turns the mesh on if it was off. On the phone, the Mac is your Desktop vault · full history.
  2. Keep Nostr Vault open on the Mac. It syncs while it's open.
  3. Shortly after the phone's vault starts, it copies your full history from the Mac on its own. After that, new posts and mentions keep syncing by themselves.

Check that it arrived

On iPhone and Android, open Settings → Your Devices. Under Your devices, your Mac's card has Sync now. It ends with what was copied and how many are missing (Done · nothing missing on iPhone, Everything copied · 0 missing on Android), or says why it stopped. The same button is under Settings → Mesh → Sync.

  • Copying your full history from the Mac…: the first copy is still running. Leave the app open.
  • Copy was interrupted: the app closed during the copy. It picks up again the next time the relay starts.

If you also have a mobile vault, its card under Your devices has Sync now too (Send now under Mesh → Sync): it sends what's waiting for it.

Good to know

  • Nothing goes through a server of ours. The phone and the Mac talk over the mesh, end to end encrypted.
  • Your phone sends your new posts to your public relays itself. When the Mac is closed or out of reach, nothing changes for them; the Mac catches up when it's back.
  • Posting a photo still needs a public media server. The Mac gets a copy as well.

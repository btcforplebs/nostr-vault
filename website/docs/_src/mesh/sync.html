Your Devices is where your linked devices live: your Macs, a Linux box, your phones, iPads and a mobile vault. Each one gets a card. Every new photo and file you add reaches all of them over the mesh, and your notes and history sync between them. It replaces the old Desktop vault sync page.

Open Your Devices

  • iPhone, iPad and Android: Settings → Your Devices.
  • Mac: Settings → Your Vault → Your Devices.

Under Your devices there is a card for each device, with its role and whether it is in reach. Your vaults have Sync now (more on that below). Connect a computer or phone (Connect a phone on the Mac) turns the mesh on if needed and starts the two scans on Link a device.

Under Advanced are Mesh (rename or remove a device there) and Desktop vault by web address, for a Mac you reach by its web address instead of the mesh.

More than one Mac

Link as many Macs and Linux boxes as you like. Each one gets its own card and its own copy of everything your phone sends, and each is queued on its own, so one Mac being off doesn't hold up the others. Remove a Mac and its role moves to the next one on the list.

Full copy, on by default

On iPhone, iPad and Android, This phone has Keep a full copy on this phone. It is on for everyone, new and existing: every photo and file from your other devices shows up here, and each new file you add goes straight to every linked device over the mesh.

  • Off: this phone keeps your notes, and fetches a file only when you open it.
  • Wi-Fi only (under it): catching up on files waits for Wi-Fi. What you add on this phone still goes out right away.
  • With Use this phone as an always-on vault on, full copy stays on. A mobile vault keeps everything.

Files show up live

When a photo or file lands on your mobile or desktop vault, the vault posts a small file note about it. Any device with its Media tab open hears that note and shows the new file at once, with no pull to refresh. Delete it everywhere and the tile goes away on the others too. Photos you save from the Media tab itself (the picker, Files, or a pasted link) go to your other devices the same way.

The vault writes that note itself for anything it is given, so uploads from the web app, Android, older app versions or other Blossom apps show up too. The note is signed with the vault's own key, never your Nostr key.

Sync now and waiting items

Anything that can't reach a device yet waits on the phone for it. The vault card shows how many are waiting, or Nothing waiting. Tap Sync now to send straight away, and on your desktop vault to check that everything arrived: it ends with what was copied and how many are missing, or says why it stopped.

The first time a new phone links to a desktop vault, it copies your full history from it on its own (Copying your full history from the Mac…; leave the app open). If the app closes, you see Copy was interrupted and it picks up the next time the vault starts. If some of the copy didn't save, it says so and tries again the next time the two devices meet.

When a device is out of reach

  • While a Mac, a Linux box or a linked phone is out of reach, the phone tries again at least every 2 minutes, and right away when the device comes back on the mesh. Nothing is dropped for being tried too often; only very old items give up.
  • A mobile vault is different: while it is out of reach the phone tries after 1 minute, then waits twice as long each time, up to 6 hours, and gives up after 40 tries.
  • Once the device answers but turns an item down, the phone backs off instead: 1 minute, then longer each time, up to 6 hours, for at most 40 tries.
  • A Mac is in reach while Nostr Vault is open on it; a phone while the app is open or in Mobile vault mode.

Your devices sign with mesh keys

Each device has its own mesh key, made on that device. What one device sends to another (photos, files, file notes) is signed with that mesh key only. Your Nostr key is never used for it, so if you sign with a remote signer (a bunker), nothing waits for you to approve it: photos go to your other devices without a tap.

Deletes travel too. Delete a photo on one device and your other devices delete their copy at the next sync. Upload it again later and it comes back.

Your vault can send your posts

When your iPhone or iPad reaches a desktop vault over the mesh, it hands each new post to the vault first. If the vault confirms within about 10 seconds, the vault sends it to your relays and the phone doesn't. If not, the phone sends it itself, as before. Either way your post goes out once. Replies and reactions still go straight to the other person's relays.

Public on the web (Mac and Linux box)

A Mac or Linux box with its own web address can be listed in your relay and media lists so anyone can reach it on the web. On the Mac, open Settings → Your Vault → Public on the web and switch on Make this vault public on the web. It is off by default; off, your devices still reach it over the mesh. A Linux box has the same switch on its own page.

  • Only one of your Macs is public on the web at a time. Turning it on while another Mac has it asks Move public web from … to …?. Tap Move and your lists name this Mac instead; the other one turns its switch off.
  • The public Mac shows Public on the web on its card. It tells your other devices its web address over the mesh, so they can use it. That address is never put in a public post by this; only the switch lists it.
  • Listing a vault in your media servers over the mesh is a separate choice, under Settings → Media Servers → Your Vaults.

Good to know

  • Nothing goes through a server of ours. Your devices talk over the mesh, end to end encrypted.
  • Posting a photo still needs a public media server. Your vaults get a copy as well.
  • The web app has no Your Devices page. It links from the Your devices card on its Vault tab.

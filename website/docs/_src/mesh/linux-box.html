A Linux box (see Get started on Linux) joins your devices like a Mac does, but it has no camera and no code to scan. You add it once, by its mesh address, from your phone or Mac.

Link the box

  1. Get the box's address. On the box's page (Settings → This Box) copy This box under My Devices. On a desktop, the tray's Copy box npub does the same.
  2. On your phone or Mac, open Settings → Your Devices (or Mesh settings) and tap Add a device.
  3. Tap Paste instead and paste the address.
  4. Under This is, choose what the box does. Mobile vault · new posts is the default, and what the box's page suggests: it keeps your new posts and media. Desktop vault · full history keeps all your history and syncs both ways, like a Mac.
  5. Give it a name if you like, and tap Add.

This adds the box on that device only. Do it on each phone or Mac that should use the box. The box lets in the devices your account lists, so there's nothing to approve on the box itself.

You're done when the box's page shows your device as Approved (Waiting until it has reached the box), and the box shows in reach under Your Devices.

The box's page

Open the vault's address (for example http://raspberrypi.local:8080/), sign in as the owner, and go to Settings → This Box. Only the owner's account can read it.

  • Status: how long it has run, its version, memory and disk.
  • Storage: the vault folder, space used and free, and Move vault to… another folder or drive. The box stops serving while it copies.
  • Mesh: whether the mesh is connected, how many peers, and the box's Mesh address.
  • My Devices: the box's own address, and each of your devices that has reached it.
  • Public on the web: for a box with its own web address, Make this vault public on the web puts it in your relay list and DM inbox. A box with no web address is reached only by your own devices over the mesh. More on Public on the web.
  • Update: Check for update and install. In the beta, updates are manual: run the installer again on the box.

Keys: what the box holds

  • The box never holds your secret key. It knows only your npub, which says whose vault it is.
  • On its first start it makes its own mesh key, readable only by the mesh service. That key is how your devices recognise the box.
  • Your own devices reach the whole vault. Anyone else on the mesh reaches only a small, rate-limited door. Nothing publishes your home IP address.

What runs on the box

For the curious: the package installs three system services.

  • nostr-vault runs havend, the vault: your relay, the media server, the web app and the box's page, on port 8080. It runs the relay engine on the machine itself, on port 3355.
  • nostr-vault-mesh runs the mesh, with the same settings as the Mac app.
  • nostr-vault-prepare sets up the vault folder once at start.

Each runs as its own user with no special rights, on a read-only system with only its own folders writable. Settings are in /etc/nostr-vault/vault.env (no secrets in it). The details are in linux/native/README.md.

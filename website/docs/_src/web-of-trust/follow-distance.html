Who can reach you

Follow Distance

A hop is one follow away from you. Your follows are 1 hop out. The people they follow are 2 hops out.

Follow DistanceWho can reach your inbox and messages
Only youOnly you and your other accounts on this device. Everyone else is kept out.
1 hopAlso the people you follow.
2 hops (default)Also the people your follows follow, once at least Minimum Followers of your follows (3 by default) follow them.

Anyone can still reply to or quote your own posts, at every setting. Your own posting is never affected.

The same web decides your feeds, notifications and the WoT globe, so a lower Follow Distance also means fewer strangers there. See What your web decides.

Why it works

Fewer hops, less spam

Spam comes from new keys that nobody follows. A spammer can make a thousand of them in a minute, but they can't make your friends follow them. So the closer to you the line is, the fewer of those keys can get in.

  • Each hop out adds many more people. You might follow 200 people, and they might follow 50,000 between them.
  • Minimum Followers guards the 2-hop ring: one careless follow by one friend isn't enough. Raise it to keep out more, lower it to let more friends of friends in.
  • Follow Distance stops at 2 hops. Nobody 3 or more hops out reaches your inbox, unless they reply to you.

Not getting a mention you expected? The sender is probably too far out. Follow them, or lower Minimum Followers, and wait for the next rebuild.

More than one account

Each account has its own

Follow Distance and Minimum Followers belong to the account you're on. Switch account and the screen shows that account's values; changing them changes that account only. With more than one account, the screen says: "These two settings are for the account you are on. Each of your accounts has its own."

  • Each account's web is built from its own follows only. Another account's follows don't let anyone in.
  • Your own accounts on the device can always reach each other, at every setting.
  • Refresh Trust List (how often the web is rebuilt) is one setting for the whole device.
  • A change restarts your vault, and the new web is ready after it rebuilds. Until then the app uses the last one it built.

Past 2 hops

Hop 3, Hop 4, Hop 5 and the trust rank

Your phone can't rank the hundreds of thousands of people further out. A desktop Vault (Nostr Vault on a Mac, or a Linux box) can. It gives people up to 6 hops out a score from 0 to 100, based on who in your web follows them, and sends the scores to your phone over the mesh. A Mac does this for itself.

  • On the WoT globe, Hop 3, Hop 4 and Hop 5 in the layer menu show each far hop's best-ranked people, labelled Found by your desktop Vault. Without the scores they're greyed: Needs a desktop Vault. This is for looking around only: it lets nobody into your inbox. More in The far rings.
  • The Discovery Reach slider picks the lowest score you'll see. People your desktop Vault ranked at that score or higher also show in Underrated and Discover, even when they're past your web. Lower lets more in. Under the slider the app counts them, for example "1,204 people in your circle and 87 distant connections": your circle is 1 and 2 hops out, distant connections are 3 and more.
  • Notifications, replies, your inbox and every other feed stay with your web, wherever the slider is.
  • With no scores yet, Discovery Reach says "Scores come from Nostr Vault on your desktop." and, with no desktop paired, "Pair a desktop Vault to see distant connections". If you put the globe's invite away, Show the invite on the globe here brings it back.

Each feed also has its own Reach in Settings → Feed: Your web, or your web plus a few people one hop out. See Feeds.

In a browser

The web app

The web app has no Follow Distance or Discovery Reach yet. Its web is always 2 hops: the Vault Dashboard says "People within 2 hops of you." Settings → Who Can Reach You has Minimum Followers and Refresh Trust List, kept in that browser.

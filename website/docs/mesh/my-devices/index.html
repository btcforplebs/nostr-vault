    Mesh and your devices

    My Devices

    On this page

    • Your Devices
    • Find Mesh settings
    • Link a device
    • Copy your vault to a new device
    • The list
    • Next

    My Devices is the list of your own phones, iPads, Macs, mobile vault and browser vault that reach each other over the mesh. They don't need a public IP or a domain: the mesh finds each device by its key, at home on Wi-Fi or out on mobile data.

    Devices on the list share your posts and media with each other. Anyone not on the list can only read your public notes and the photos in them, and leave you a message if your inbox lets them.

    Your Devices

    The quickest way in is Settings → Your Devices on iPhone, iPad and Android, and Settings → Your Vault → Your Devices on the Mac. It shows a card for each of your devices, with Sync now on your vaults, and Connect a computer or phone (Connect a phone on the Mac), which turns the mesh on if needed and starts the two scans below. The mesh's own settings are under Advanced there, and in the places listed next. What syncs and how is on Your Devices and sync.

    Find Mesh settings

    • iPhone and iPad: the Mesh button at the top of the Profile tab, next to Settings. Or hold your profile picture in the tab bar and pick Mesh settings.
    • Android: hold your account picture and pick Mesh settings, or Settings → Your Devices → Mesh.
    • Mac: Settings → Your Vault → Mesh, or Mesh settings under Advanced on the Vault dashboard.
    • Everywhere, the Vault dashboard has a Your devices card with Mesh settings and Add.

    Switch on Mesh (connecting a device from Your Devices does this for you). This Device shows this device's Mesh address, a long key starting with npub.

    Link a device

    Two devices link with two scans: one shows a code, the other scans it, then they swap. Both end up on each other's list, so you only do it once.

    1. On both devices, turn the mesh on, or start from Connect a computer or phone in Your Devices, which turns it on. On one of them tap Link a device under This Device, or Add under Your devices.
    2. iPhone, iPad and Mac: tap Show my code on one device and Scan a code on the other. Android opens on its own code; tap Scan its code to scan the other one first, or Show this phone's code instead to go back. Web: the Your devices card has Show my code and Scan a code.
    3. The first device scans the second one's screen back. Each code lasts a few minutes.
    4. Check the words. Both devices show the same four words. If they match, tap They match (on Android, Do these words match?; tap They don't match if they don't). A browser with no camera links by the words alone.
    5. Confirm with Add (Android: Add it). The screen says Linked ✓ and the device's name. Tap Done.

    No camera? On iPhone, iPad and Mac every scan step has Paste instead, and every code also shows as text with Copy. A Linux box or the browser relay page has no code, only a mesh address: paste that under Paste instead. To add this device to one of those, copy its address from Mesh → Advanced (Copy mesh address on the Mac, Copy address on Android).

    Something went wrong? Start again shows a fresh code.

    You're done when the other device shows a green dot under Your devices. It can take a moment once both are on the mesh.

    Copy your vault to a new device

    Setting up a new phone, iPad or Mac? Choose Copy from my other vault when setup asks New to Nostr Vault? The two devices link with two scans as above, then the new one copies the whole vault across over the mesh: your notes, photos and videos, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. Every account on the old device that can sign is offered too. Keep Nostr Vault open on the old device while it copies. If it can't be reached, the new one tries again every 15 seconds for five minutes, then offers Try again. The steps are on iPhone and Android. The web app links the same way, but takes your notes from your relays.

    The list

    • Under My Devices, a phone shows each device's role and whether it is connected now, or Not in reach. Android shows In reach or when it was last seen. A Mac is in reach while Nostr Vault is open on it; a phone while the app is open or in Mobile vault mode.
    • A linked Mac is a Desktop vault · full history: your new notes and photos go to it, and your history syncs back. You can link more than one Mac; each gets its own copy. Your phones also send each new photo and file to every linked device. See Your Devices and sync.
    • Tap a device to rename it, copy its address, or remove it with Remove from My Devices (you confirm first). Android also shows when it was Last seen.
    • Once a device is on the list, only the devices on the list learn this phone's address. Anyone else on the mesh gets public photos only.

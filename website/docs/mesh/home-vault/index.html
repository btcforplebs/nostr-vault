    Mesh and your devices

    Mobile and desktop vaults

    On this page

    • What each kind does
    • 1. Turn on Mobile vault mode on the spare phone
    • 2. Add it to My Devices
    • Where new photos go

    Your mobile or desktop vault is the vault that stays on. A desktop vault is a Mac or a Linux box. A mobile vault is a box or an old phone on a charger. Your other devices reach it over the mesh, so your notes and media have a home even when your phone is off.

    What each kind does

    • Desktop vault · full history (a Mac or a Linux box): keeps all your history and syncs both ways with your phones. You can have more than one. See Your Devices and sync.
    • Mobile vault · new posts (a spare phone or a box): keeps a copy of your new posts and media, and a full copy of every file (Mobile vault mode keeps Keep a full copy on this phone on). Your phone still sends your notes to your relays itself. This page shows how to set one up on a spare phone.

    1. Turn on Mobile vault mode on the spare phone

    1. Install Nostr Vault on the spare phone and sign in with the same account.
    2. On iPhone, add your main phone under My Devices first (step 2). Mobile vault mode won't start with an empty list.
    3. Open Settings → Your Devices and, under This phone, switch on Use this phone as an always-on vault. It is the same switch as Mobile vault mode under Settings → Mesh → Share With Others.
    4. Leave it on a charger.
    • On iPhone, Mobile vault mode keeps the screen on, pauses when you leave the app and turns on again when you come back. On Android it keeps serving in the background while your vault runs, and after a restart.
    • Your devices in My Devices reach the mobile vault in full.
    • Anyone else on the mesh can read your public notes and the photos in your posts, and slowly: 1 MB/s for all of them together, at most 256 MB each. They get 1 GB a day in total, then wait until the next day: Share With Others shows Sharing paused and when it starts again. Your own devices are never limited. They can't read your messages or private notes, or change anything.

    2. Add it to My Devices

    1. The quickest way: on both phones tap Link a device and do the two scans (see Link a device). Or add it by address, as below. On the spare phone, under This Device, copy its mesh address.
    2. On your main phone, open Settings → Mesh → Add a device. Under Paste instead, paste the address, tap Use, and pick Mobile vault · new posts.
    3. Add the main phone to the spare phone's My Devices too.

    From then on, your new notes and media also go to the mobile vault over the mesh, as a copy: your main phone still sends your notes to your relays itself, and keeps them too. Anything that can't reach the mobile vault waits on the main phone and goes when the app is open and the mobile vault is in reach: the phone tries at least every 2 minutes while it is out of reach, and right away when it comes back. Sync now on its card in Your Devices (Send now under Mesh → Sync) sends it straight away.

    What's public. Linking a vault doesn't list it. If you add it under Settings → Media Servers → Your Vaults, its mesh address is listed in your media server list, in public, so other apps can fetch your photos from it. Direct-message attachments are never sent there.

    Media goes to the mobile vault signed with this phone's mesh key, so it goes without a tap even if your key is in a remote signer.

    Where new photos go

    New photos are saved on this device first. If you have a mobile vault, a copy goes to it over the mesh, and the mobile vault puts it on your public media server for you. If this device has its own media server, it uploads there itself.

    Checked against Nostr Vault 3.2.2.

Common questions

I lost my secret key. Can you get it back?

No. Your secret key (it starts with nsec) is kept only on your device, or in your signer app. It is never sent to us or any server, so nobody can reset or recover it, not even us.

  • If another device still has the account, open Settings → Accounts & Keys there and back up the key now.
  • If you saved the key, or a password-locked copy (starts with ncryptsec), add it again in Settings → Accounts & Keys → Add Account.
  • If the key is gone everywhere, that account can't post again. Create a new account, and tell the people who follow you your new npub.

If you forgot the password that locks your key on a device, your saved secret key is the only way back in. See Your keys and accounts.

How do I delete my account?

Your Nostr account is a pair of keys that lives on your devices, not on our servers, so removing it from the app removes it from that device:

  • One account: Settings → Accounts & Keys → tap the account → Remove Account.
  • Everything on this device (all accounts, keys, your vault and its notes): Settings → Database & Reset → Factory Reset. On the web app, Sign out on your last account does the same for that browser.

Notes you already published are also on the public relays you posted to. You can delete a note from its menu in the app, which asks those relays to remove it. To have anything removed from our relay, relay.btcforplebs.com, email us from the contact below with your npub.

My notes or follows don't show on another device. How does sync work?

Your notes, follows and profile are kept on your Nostr relays, so any device signed in to the same account sees them. Each device's vault also keeps its own copy.

  • Make sure both devices use the same relays: Settings → Relays.
  • To fill a new device with your older notes, use Settings → Your Vault → Import Notes (web app: Settings → Import Notes).
  • To move a whole vault, use Settings → Your Vault → Backup & Restore (web app: Settings → Backup & Restore), or link your devices in My Devices.

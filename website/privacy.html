Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the Nostr Vault application ("the App"). Nostr Vault is a decentralized social media client and local relay for the Nostr network.

1. Data Collection and Processing

Nostr Vault operates as a local node on your device. The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, analytics, or telemetry on external servers controlled by the developer.

2. Nostr Network Usage

As a Nostr client, the App interacts with decentralized relays around the world. When you use the App to publish notes, update your profile, or send messages, that data is cryptographically signed and broadcast to the relays you configure. This information becomes public (or encrypted in the case of direct messages) on the Nostr network, governed by the protocol itself.

3. Local Storage

All of your data, including your private keys (nsec/ncryptsec), config files, and downloaded feed data, are stored securely and directly on your local device. Private keys are securely encrypted using NIP-49 algorithms and stored in your device's Keychain or secure enclave where applicable.

4. User Generated Content (UGC)

The App displays User Generated Content from the Nostr network. You have full control over the content you see through local whitelists, blacklists, and manual relay configurations. The App provides tools to locally block abusive users or report them to the developer for potential global filtering.

5. The Web App and nostrvault.app

The web app at nostrvault.app/vault/ runs in your browser. Its relay, your notes, messages and drafts, a bunker connection (stored encrypted) and the browser's own FIPS mesh key are kept in that browser's storage on your device. It never holds your secret key: your browser extension or bunker does. Signing out deletes what the browser keeps, and the browser may delete it too (Safari does after 7 days without a visit).

  • Relays and services you use. Your browser connects directly to your Nostr relays (relay.damus.io and relay.primal.net when you have none of your own, and purplepag.es for profiles), to the servers that host the photos and videos you view or upload, and to people's zap services when you zap or when zaps are counted. Like any website, each of them sees your IP address.
  • FIPS mesh seeds (iris.to). When you start FIPS on the Relay screen, or a photo is loaded from someone's vault over the FIPS mesh, the page connects to the public seeds fips1.iris.to and fips2.iris.to. They are run by iris.to, not by Nostr Vault. They see your IP address and the browser's mesh address, which is not your Nostr account. You can turn mesh photos off in Settings → Media Servers → Load photos over FIPS mesh.
  • The website. nostrvault.app is served through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and the pages you request. Our server keeps a standard web server log (IP address, page and time). There are no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Shared post pages. When someone opens a nostrvault.app/thread/ link, our server reads that public post from public Nostr relays and keeps the page in memory for up to 10 minutes.
